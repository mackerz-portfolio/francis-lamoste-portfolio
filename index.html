DESIGN SPECIALTIES

Built for the page.

Publishing design is where visual storytelling meets practical craft. I aim to make every page feel considered, consistent, and easy to read.

Book Cover DesignInterior LayoutTypography & HierarchyPrint-Ready FilesCover ConceptsAdobe InDesign

My process: understand the brief and audience, establish a clear visual direction, refine the details through feedback, then prepare final artwork with production requirements in mind.

01 / DISCOVER

Understand the story

Review the brief, genre, audience, author goals, and any publishing specifications before choosing a direction.

02 / DESIGN

Build a clear visual system

Shape typography, imagery, hierarchy, and page rhythm so the design feels cohesive and intentional.

03 / DELIVER

Polish for production

Refine from feedback and prepare organized, accurate files suited to the project’s print or digital needs.

02 — ABOUT THE DESIGNER

Publishing meets purpose.

With more than two decades in graphic design, I bring a detail-focused approach to publishing projects, from developing a cover concept and establishing typography to preparing polished, production-ready files. I work to make each design visually engaging while keeping the author’s message and audience at the center.

My experience includes book cover design, interior layout, typography, print production, branding, and packaging. I use Adobe Photoshop, Illustrator, and InDesign to build cohesive designs and carefully prepared files.
